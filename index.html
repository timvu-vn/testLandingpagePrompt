Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about restoring your precious memories

How does photo restoration work?

Simply upload your old or damaged photo, and our AI technology automatically detects and fixes issues like scratches, tears, fading, and blur. The process takes just a few seconds, and you'll receive a beautifully restored version ready to download.

Is it really free to use?

Yes! You can restore up to 5 photos per month completely free. For unlimited restorations and access to premium features like batch processing and priority support, we offer affordable subscription plans starting at just $9.99/month.

What photo formats do you support?

Memori supports all common image formats including JPG, PNG, TIFF, and even scanned photos. For best results, we recommend uploading the highest quality version of your photo available.

Are my photos safe and private?

Absolutely. We take your privacy seriously. All photos are encrypted during upload and processing. After restoration, your photos are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours. We never share or sell your images to third parties.
